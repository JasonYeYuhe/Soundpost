• Soundpost can now recognise everyday sounds — rain, birdsong, a train — so a capsule is easier to find months later. Search "rain" and your rainy mornings come back.
• After a recording, it offers what it heard as a suggestion for your one line. Take it or ignore it; nothing is ever filled in for you.
• The listening happens on your device — no audio is sent away to be analysed, and what it hears stays with the capsule, syncing only to your own private iCloud. You can turn listening off in Settings, which also erases what it already heard.

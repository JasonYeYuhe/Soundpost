• What Soundpost heard is now on the capsule itself. It has recognised sounds on your device since 1.6.0 and then kept them to itself — now your capsule says so, in your language, and always as what it is: something Soundpost heard, never a claim about your memory.
• Tap a sound to find the other capsules that sounded like it.
• You can change a capsule after saving it — your one line, the mood, the place's name, the echo date. The recording itself, and when you made it, stay as they were.
• Play a capsule straight from the gallery.
• A recording in progress can no longer be lost to a stray swipe, and throwing one away asks first.
• The listening setting now follows your iCloud account instead of a single phone. Turn it off anywhere and it goes off on your other devices too — and what Soundpost had already heard is erased from them as well. Each device applies it the next time you open it.
• Two messages in Settings that had been appearing in English now show in your own language.
